# Community Guidelines

FixAtlas is a collaborative project. Participants are expected to be respectful, patient, and constructive.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, threats, and publishing another person's private information. Technical disagreement is welcome; hostility is not.

Maintainers may edit or remove content that violates these guidelines and may restrict participation when necessary to keep the community safe and useful.

Report concerns privately through the repository's available reporting channel.
